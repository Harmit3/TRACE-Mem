# Independent validation protocol v2

**Pending independent review and explicit project approval.** This proposal follows the examined Step 30/31 outcomes. No final histories exist and no inference or controller fitting was performed. Step 35's original proposal and all prior artifacts remain as written.

The objective is to estimate how T2 performs relative to B3 and H-paced on evolving, source-authorized temporal state under a common two-call ceiling. Use 90 independently reviewed mechanism families with four histories each. Freeze the final eligibility set, authoring and validation logs, complete cohort digest, backend identity and code before execution. The plan assumes planning SD 0.20 and a five-point interval precision target. [Analysis plan](statistical_analysis_plan.md)

## Sequence

1. Obtain independent academic review of this protocol and the mechanism archive. Require separate fixture author and gold reviewer. Resolve representational incompatibilities before authoring final examples. Approve a protocol revision through the blank form.
2. In a later authorized step, construct the cohort. Run deterministic schema and graph checks, freeze every exclusion and gold label, and obtain independent gold sign-off before evaluating any candidate. Reject duplicate mechanisms even when their prose differs.
3. Run the approved executable freeze with sorted families and history IDs. Use B3, T2, H-paced and T2-no-NLI, with two calls as the primary ceiling and zero and five secondary. Keep fixed-stream exact-quota diagnostics separate. If compliance restrictions stop spending, record the actual attempts and reason. No hidden quota refill.
4. Apply separate fixed scoring and no post-hoc exclusions. Report family-bootstrap intervals, failure-inclusive state and reader outcomes, harmful changes, actual calls, logical tokens, physical calls and cache expenditure. Treat missing authorization as failure and halt access to restricted evidence.

## Calendar planner freeze candidate

The Step 23 development fixtures have 20 prepared eligible arrivals over two scheduled six-day observation windows from the first candidate day through the final check date. Use their pooled 20/12 arrivals per day as the prior mean and three pseudo-days, half one development window, for Gamma shape 5 and rate 3. This is a prespecified weak contextual prior, selected from those examined fixtures without fitting against Step 30/31 or future outcomes. A family distribution with different timelines can shift its accuracy.

Before each eligible opportunity on day d, let E = d minus opening day, R = closing day minus d, and n = previously observed eligible opportunities strictly before the current item in chronological order. Same-day predecessors count toward n. Forecast clamp(round-half-up((5+n) × R / (3+E)), 0, 20), then give the unchanged pacing logic the total horizon n + 1 + forecast. The two secondary stress multipliers 0.5 and 2 apply before rounding and bounding. These are candidate protocol parameters and are not tuned on final histories. Use the announced window even if there are no arrivals. With no candidate there is no verification decision. Sparse arrivals shrink the rate over exposure, bursts are bounded, and the closing-day forecast is zero. Exhausted call caps remain exhausted even during a burst. An overestimated horizon can cause underspending. Record it rather than adjusting rules retrospectively.

The wrapper receives only the current day, elapsed calendar, prior eligible count, remaining allowance and current candidate score. Eligibility is based on each policy's own prior state. Future observed arrivals are calculated retrospectively from the trace. The fixed diagnostic uses future reference opportunities by design and never contributes to the chronological estimand.

## Execution state

`trace_mem.validation_v2.run_reviewed` is the programmatic entry point for an approved synthetic package. It accepts an immutable episode projection plus a separate gold object, three distinct role receipts, frozen cohort digest and backend fingerprint. The receipt is a recorded assertion. Independent verification of identities and independence happens outside the runner. No approved manifest, backend wiring or final cohort is included. The project must validate the frozen adapter, feature order and controller digests before execution. The runner executes eligible conflicts with the existing transition rules and pacing logic, uses a keyed SQLite verifier ledger, checkpoints case-condition outputs, and reproduces order on resume. Run one writer per output directory. A pending attempted dispatch is retained in the physical ledger if interrupted. It cannot establish whether a timed-out transport consumed tokens, so unknown usage stays explicitly unmetered.

The infrastructure emits the nominal forecasts; half and double stress ratios are exposed by the replay wrapper for separately scheduled secondary runs. It scores six current-state checks using end-of-observation-day snapshots and five historical queries with separate observation and effective dates. Only retained and authorized facts are indexed, unresolved deletion requests quarantine the episode, and answer citations are checked against the authorized materialization. The projected reader receives no gold. Stale fixed targets consume a call without applying their verdict. Record unauthenticated or failed execution separately from approved scientific results. Thresholds, original policy implementations and original benchmarks remain frozen.
