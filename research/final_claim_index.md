# Final claim-to-evidence index

| Claim | Evidence | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learned allocation has conditional chronological benefits | Step 30 T2 vs H-paced at 250 calls each, +6.7 pp, paired 95% interval +0.7 to +12.3 | 100 synthetic histories grouped in ten design families. The nominal 50% T2 vs B3 interval crosses zero. |
| Ranking reverses on the matched stream | Step 30 T2 vs H-paced, −11.3 pp at 250 calls each, interval −16.3 to −6.3 | Fixed targets can become stale. Timing and state co-vary, so stale targets were not isolated experimentally. |
| Horizon overestimation can reduce spending and accuracy | Step 31 at 50%, doubled horizon, T2 spent 211/250 and scored 59.0%; B3 spent 250 and scored 68.7% | Post-hoc robustness on the Step 30 histories. |
| Internal state gains do not guarantee answer gains | Step 31 nominal 50% reader subset, T2 7/30, B3 6/30, H-paced 12/30 | Small reused cohort with reader and evidence bottlenecks. |
| External superiority is unestablished | Step 32 managed replay completed one history out of 14 and missed it. BM25 3/14, MiniLM 2/14, oracle 6/14 under local scoring | Differences in completion and evidence access preclude a controller superiority estimate. Step 33 candidates missed frozen gates. |
| Human-assisted field enforcement has bounded evidence | Step 29 reviewed grants and frozen field safety fixtures | Step 27 released zero real targets autonomously. Limited checks do not establish comprehensive privacy. |
| The published arithmetic is reproducible offline | Step 35 sanitized counts and independent implementation of aggregation and family bootstrap | Regeneration verifies figures from released observations. Independent scientific replication and the proposed 90-family study remain pending. |
