# TRACE-Mem independent study overview

**For academic review. The protocol and cohort are pending independent review and explicit approval.**

Long-running assistants accumulate facts that can become stale. Additional verification costs time and tokens, and accepting a correction can change which fact a later update would replace. TRACE-Mem tests when a learned call allocator improves temporal state within a fixed verification allowance.

On the same 100 fictional histories in ten authored families, T2 led a paced heuristic by 6.7 points at 250 calls in chronological replay and trailed it by 11.3 points on a matched fixed stream. The latter can spend calls on targets that are no longer live. On the reused cohort, T2 did not consistently beat an early-call baseline, and overestimating arrivals caused underspend and lower accuracy. The reader subset showed no corresponding short-answer advantage. Only one of 14 external LongMemEval diagnostic histories qualified for managed comparison, and all arms missed the answer. The public Step 35 release reproduces the saved statistics without model calls. It is an internal reproducibility check.

The proposed new study compares frozen T2 with B3 and H-paced under the same **two-call ceiling per history**, with T2-no-NLI as a secondary comparison. It aims for 90 structurally distinct families and four histories each. The two primary contrasts are paired at the family level, using adjusted bootstrap intervals. Six exact authorized current-state checks per history form the primary denominator. Historical accuracy, failure-inclusive reader outcomes, stale targets and actual call and token expenditure remain separate. The causal pacing wrapper uses prior eligible arrivals and the preannounced calendar exposure.

The key scientific need is independently authored and reviewed mechanisms and gold, including an assessment that varied surface wording does not manufacture independent families. Prepared claims focus the experiment on verification and state progression, so it will not measure extractor performance. The frozen engine uses a single subject, exact slot conflicts and day-granularity validity. It cannot adjudicate arbitrary concurrent writes, world knowledge or ambiguous deletion scope.

We seek substantive review of the design, fixture independence and gold protocol. Participation, scope and credit would be agreed separately. No reviewer, final history, signature or new result exists yet. [Onboarding and review materials](reviewer_onboarding.md)
