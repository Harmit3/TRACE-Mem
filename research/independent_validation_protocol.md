# Proposed independent validation protocol

**Status: pending independent review and explicit approval.** This design follows examined Steps 30 and 31. No evaluation fixtures have been generated, assigned, or run. The findings motivating the design remain exploratory. Reviewers should approve a frozen revision before anyone generates the final evaluation set or runs verification.

## Cohort and independent review

Recruit an independent fixture author and a separate reviewer. Give them a specification of supported state operations and data boundaries, with policy names, prior example text and policy outputs withheld during gold review. Preserve a versioned conflict-mechanism taxonomy, independently checked gold timelines, mutation records, and reasons for excluding invalid fixtures before unblinding.

Require each family to vary the source of contradiction and dependency structure, with substantive differences from one another and from all training, calibration and examined evaluation families. A new name, domain setting or paraphrase alone cannot establish disjointness. Consider mechanisms such as interrupted chains of contingent obligations, simultaneous independent subentity histories with later disambiguation, source authority that changes by scope, and delayed corrections with validity gaps. These are design directions, subject to a review of whether the frozen system can represent the mechanism. Independently compare candidate family specifications with all prior design taxonomies and generator mechanisms, and screen normalized text and state-transition graphs for structural overlap. Freeze generation and exclusion rules before scoring. Keep the Memora and LongMemEval reserves unopened. Derive gold current, historical, erroneous proposal, and authorized visibility outcomes without model responses. Resolve disagreements through a separate blinded adjudicator and log unresolved items as such.

## Interval precision and sample size

The unit of inference is a conflict family, averaged equally. Fix four histories per family initially to check execution variance, including different arrival patterns. Increasing histories within a family cannot substitute for family diversity. At the nominal 50% condition in the examined cohort, the sample SDs of the paired family means were 0.091 for T2 minus B3 and 0.101 for T2 minus H-paced. Those estimates come from only ten selected clusters. Use a conservative planning SD of 0.20 in absolute accuracy units and a target half-width of 0.05. With two primary contrasts, a Bonferroni 97.5% interval has a normal critical value near 2.24, yielding `ceil((2.24*0.20/0.05)^2) = 81` families. The finite-sample t critical value near 2.28 implies about 84. Plan **90 genuinely distinct families**, approximately 360 histories, to provide margin for interval precision. This is a planning calculation, conditional on the variance assumption, and does not guarantee power. Publish achieved interval widths. If the independently reviewed eligible set is smaller than the target, report the shortfall and narrow the inference.

## Frozen policies and estimands

Use the existing digested T2 score and pacing logic, B3 early-call policy, H-paced and T2-no-NLI. Preserve controller weights, cutoffs, calibration, feature ordering and verifier settings. Declare a fixed two-call ceiling per history as the primary condition, known in advance and independent of arrivals. Declare five calls and zero calls as secondary conditions. Do not replenish after errors or transfer unused calls between histories.

The co-primary comparisons are T2 minus B3 and T2 minus H-paced on mean chronological exact-state accuracy under the same two-call ceiling. Use a common predeclared schedule of as-of checkpoints and final-state checks, averaging within history and then within family. Bootstrap families with one seed, 10,000 draws and simultaneous Bonferroni-adjusted two-sided percentile intervals for the two primary contrasts. Report actual attempts and secondary paired history accuracy, harmful and helpful outcomes, T2 without NLI, strict fixed-stream quota comparisons on frozen reference proposals (separate from chronological replay), and token latency. Chronological equal-cap contrasts retain underspending and variance in eligibility. A descriptive equal-realized-cost subset is secondary and potentially selection-biased. Do not filter the primary outcome by policy completion or matching spend after observing results.

For reader outcomes, declare question selection and supported/refuted/insufficient annotations before predictions. Report complete-case accuracy and the primary failure-inclusive reader denominator separately. For every planned episode, score absent output, timeout, terminal parse failure and unauthorized retrieval as failure. Unanswered but correctly adjudicated abstention counts as correct only when the gold task is unanswerable. Track invalid sources and wrong presence or forgetting separately. Include availability of annotated evidence after retrieval, reader behavior given sufficient evidence, and the restriction gate outcome. If a pre-score gold item is irresolvable, publish the frozen exclusion and count. Never turn model failure into an exclusion.

## Causal planning and diagnostics

Give each replay a preannounced calendar window. Let elapsed exposure be measured through the current turn. Estimate the candidate intensity from past observable arrivals, using a positive Gamma prior whose parameters are selected only on a separate non-final development archive and frozen before final generation. Update its posterior mean with observations strictly before the current candidate, and multiply by remaining scheduled calendar exposure. Round and bound the projected remaining count using prespecified bounds. Pass the resulting nominal horizon (arrived count including the current candidate plus estimated future count) to the existing pacing mechanism. T2 and H-paced share the same observable stream rules and prior, and each sees its own eligible arrivals so far. The verifier then sees current authorized evidence only. Neither the plan nor caps receive future actual arrivals or gold conflicts. If no defensible preannounced calendar window exists, freeze a fixed development-derived horizon as the primary approach before running anything. Do not choose between these approaches after scoring.

At each decision, log time, planned remaining opportunities, actual remaining counted only after replay, eligibility and frozen-versus-live target identity, decision rank, whether a call was submitted, accepted application to the current target, malformed/time-out outcome, actual cache status and token usage. Record forecast bias, absolute errors and utilization per family. Freeze forecast multipliers for over- and under-estimation as secondary stress tests. Keep exact-budget fixed-stream results separate, including stale calls expended without application. Present both physical model calls and logical requests, and track reader schema errors, ground-truth disagreement and abstention. Independent reviewers approve protocol scope, disjointness, numeric tolerances, gold rubric, safety gates, and an archival plan before execution.
