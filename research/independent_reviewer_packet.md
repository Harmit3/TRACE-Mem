# Independent reviewer packet

**For an independent academic reviewer. Participation, judgement and approval are pending.**

The proposed question is whether learned pacing improves chronological authorized temporal-state accuracy under the same two-call ceiling as early-call and heuristic pacing. The original evidence comes from one examined ten-family cohort. Prior positive and negative outcomes motivated the design. They do not constitute confirmatory evidence for the new protocol.

## Instructions for the fixture author

Use the [authoring specification](fixture_authoring_specification.md) and [mechanism taxonomy](conflict_mechanism_taxonomy.md). Before generating final cases, obtain protocol approval. Later draft mechanism graphs and keep this gold work separate from policy implementation. Submit opaque family/history IDs, narrative source excerpts, causal graph and calendar, gold checks and reasons for exclusions. Provide no policy predictions or score labels to the gold reviewer. Document AI involvement, including whether AI assisted wording.

## Instructions for the independent reviewer

1. Review the [protocol](validation_protocol_v2.md), [analysis plan](statistical_analysis_plan.md), and its sample-size sensitivity and failure denominator.
2. Independently assess structural disjointness, case feasibility and date semantics from authorized, de-identified source material. At the gold-review stage withhold policy identities, policy outcomes, any automatic verification responses and published predictions.
3. Check authorization, target separability, expected historical visibility and whether reader questions have independently defensible answers. Keep unresolved cases pending. Record disagreements and adjudication on the blank form.
4. Review the freeze manifest with the approving project owner before execution. Review the analyzed policy mapping only after gold and the negative-result retention plan are locked.

This packet, schema and infrastructure are AI-assisted project materials. Project-owner approval or an internal engineering review cannot substitute for external review of the new fixtures.
