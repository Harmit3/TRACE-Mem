# Collaboration inquiry draft for project-owner review

**Subject** — Independent review of a budgeted temporal-memory evaluation

Dear [Researcher name],

I’m developing TRACE-Mem, a research prototype that studies when checking conflicting memory updates improves long-horizon LLM memory. In an exploratory synthetic study, learned pacing helped in chronological replay at one matched call budget, but ranked below a heuristic on a fixed stream. The learned policy also struggled when it overestimated how many conflicts would arrive. We have published an offline reproduction package and are preparing a separate validation study.

Would you be open to reviewing the proposed protocol or discussing a limited collaboration on the fixture design? We are particularly interested in whether the conflict families would be genuinely distinct and whether the gold timeline and failure rules are rigorous. The initial protocol review is estimated at two to four hours. A larger role in authoring or independent gold review would be discussed separately.

The one-page overview and reproducibility instructions are in the [TRACE-Mem repository](https://github.com/Harmit3/TRACE-Mem). The study remains pending review. No final cohort has been generated, and there is no expectation of participation if the scope does not fit your interests or availability.

Thank you for considering it.

[Project owner name]
