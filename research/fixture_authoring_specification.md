# Fixture authoring specification

**Review draft. Do not generate the final cohort until independent protocol approval.**

## Author instructions

Use four small fictional histories per distinct family. Log the structural mechanism and counterfactual distinguishing it from each prior neighbor. Keep domains and superficial text changes out of the novelty decision. Include routine nonconflict turns, changes valid immediately or later, trusted and ambiguous provenance, explicit uncertainty, and conditions for a verifier to improve or harm the state. Only use state operations the frozen implementation can represent. Supply reviewed operations and source-authorized verbatim evidence. Preserve source reference, observed calendar day, in-day sequence, speaker, source kind, explicit effective date and optional exclusive expiry. Use one subject with separable slots. Use explicit approved deletion targets and test that unresolved targets block access. Keep revoked values and scope rules in the restricted fixture-validation artifact rather than model inputs.

## Data contract

- Episode holds sorted `PreparedClaim` items, globally unique history/family/claim identifiers, calendar bounds announced at start, a fact allowlist, and a restricted-value gate. In-day ordering is immutable. The allowlist covers only reviewed verbatim support excerpts and the proposed value must be contained in each approved span. No restricted token string may enter a query or allowed span.
- Gold lives in a separate companion object. Annotate six current accuracy checks and five historical checks with independent observation date, effective date, predicate and exact authorized value set. Include at least one current check on the closing day. Values may be an empty set when absent by rule. For helpfulness comparisons use days with a single focal proposal per checked predicate so an immediate verification outcome can be compared to both the reviewed gold and the deterministic no-verifier transition. List harmful proposal IDs and independently identified answerable status and exact acceptable answer strings. Literal answer matching is deliberately narrow. Use short factual questions and author their acceptable forms before inference.
- Gold timeline rules use observable source order. A fact cannot appear before its observation date. A confirmed replacement closes the previous validity interval at its effective date. Temporary observations are not permanent replacements. Retain authorized past values within valid intervals. Deletion suppresses the scoped target and its derivatives, including historical retrieval when already revoked. Explicitly distinguish ambiguity from deletion and record unresolved scopes. Review both the drawing and rendering of historical/as-of views.
- For each family record the graph nodes and dependency edges, and a lexical normalization mapping from identifying tokens to stable placeholders. Exact normalized text, at least .80 five-shingle Jaccard similarity, or matching canonical state graph against any known family requires review. Exact mechanism duplicates fail; new graph hashes alone do not establish semantic independence.

## Validation and exclusions

Machine checks require chronological observations, unique IDs, explicit allowed sources, supported spans, source isolation, fixed cardinalities, valid calendar and query cutoffs, distinct checkpoints, distinct nonblank expected values, and answerability consistent with allowed answers. CI tests prove schema and access boundaries only. Independent reviewers must separately check that the timeline is feasible, annotations match narrative evidence, prohibited fragments or derivatives cannot be recovered, and graph labels represent the stated dependency. Each supported gold reader outcome must have an authorized supporting path.

Before unblinding exclude unresolved gold, insufficient provenance, mislabeled authorization, near-duplicate mechanism after review, impossible timestamp/validity clocks, and unsupported state transitions. Do not exclude scarce conflicts, low scores, actual model failures, mistakes by a policy or uneven spend. Record every exclusion by opaque ID, category, decision and independent reviewer. Freeze the whole cohort and rejection ledger. A cohort below 90 accepted families cannot use the current approved-runner target.
