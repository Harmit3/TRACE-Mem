# Conflict mechanism taxonomy and disjointness rules

This archive covers previously examined synthetic training, calibration, diagnostic and evaluation mechanisms. Benchmark development entries describe observed categories only. No untouched reserve was inspected. For final eligibility, reviewers must extend the table into a per-family registry from the listed source manifests before comparing candidate mechanisms.

| --- | --- |

Require novelty in an outcome-relevant causal structure plus at least one other dimension such as temporal applicability, evidence authority, cross-slot dependency, or authorization lineage. State the event whose removal or reordered causal relationship would change gold. A renaming, occupation change, new domain, paraphrase, timestamp shift or repeated template cannot satisfy that requirement. Use the canonical graph of entity/slot/value recurrence, operation, temporal relation, source authority and directed dependencies. An exact canonical duplicate fails; normalized text or 5-shingle Jaccard at least 0.80 triggers review. The code detects controlled renaming and exact ordered graph duplicates. It does not prove semantic independence or graph isomorphism under arbitrary reorderings. Two humans review near matches and rule-level equivalence. Any purported new mechanism outside the frozen representable state transitions is excluded before predictions and logged.
