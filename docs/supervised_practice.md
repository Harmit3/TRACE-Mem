# Supervised practice and usability study preparation

## Owner rehearsal

Use **Open practice profile** in the desktop launcher. The separate Practice directory is under the current user's TRACE-Mem Beta profile. Open the guided practice panel and choose Workshop planner or Field researcher. A supervisor may demonstrate the tasks using fictional facts. Use the selected fictional introduction, confirm the proposal, start a second chat, ask a current question, revise the effective date, ask a historical question, then forget and delete the practice chats. Check that the evidence inspector, displayed date, and answer type agree with what the user expected. The optional comment box has no submit handler, telemetry or automatic storage. No participants were recruited and no feedback was collected in Step 42.

## Participant information sheet — draft for ethics review

The proposed activity examines whether people can understand and control a local assistant's memory. A session would take approximately 15–20 minutes. You would use an invented profile, inspect saved evidence, correct a remembered fact and delete it. You may skip any task or stop at any time. Participation is voluntary. There is no guaranteed personal benefit. A possible inconvenience is confusion about whether deletion applies to memories, transcripts or backups. The supervisor will explain these distinctions. Please do not enter your name, contact information, real experiences or sensitive facts.

Workspace runs on the study computer. Ollama processes text locally when enabled. The study plan excludes audio, video, screenshots and transcript exports. The supervisor would record only an anonymous task code, completion or difficulty and a brief optional comment you explicitly agree to share. The in-app scratchpad itself sends nothing. Do not copy it by default. The supervisor must tell you who can access any observations, how long they will be kept, what you can withdraw and until when, and whom to contact about the study or your rights before requesting consent.

## Proposed consent script — inactive

Before a formal session, give the participant the approved information sheet and time for questions. Ask separately whether they understand the tasks and local data handling, agree voluntarily to take part, and agree to any optional written feedback. Declining the last item must not prevent participation. Document consent only by the method approved for the study. Never infer consent from opening the app, choosing a profile or performing a task.

## Ethics and operational protocol

Identify the responsible institution, investigator, supervisor, independent participant contact, intended population and data custodian before recruitment. Obtain the institution's written determination concerning research ethics review. Under Canadian TCPS 2, research involving human participants generally requires prior REB review. A genuine internal improvement activity can fall outside that scope. A plan to generate research findings or reuse observations for research changes the decision. The project owner's software authorization does not authorize enrolling participants. [TCPS 2 Chapter 2](https://ethics.gc.ca/eng/tcps2-eptc2_2022_chapter2-chapitre2.html) · [Consent process](https://ethics.gc.ca/eng/tcps2-eptc2_2022_chapter3-chapitre3.html)

Institutional affiliation is unconfirmed. If this research falls under Western University, consult the Office of Human Research Ethics and Non-Medical REB. Prepare the protocol, recruitment materials, information/consent documents, privacy plan and applicable training before submission. Resolve approval, retention, withdrawal deadlines and actual contacts before circulating participant materials. [Western submissions](https://www.uwo.ca/research/compliance/human-research-ethics/submissions.html)

For an approved session, use a fresh isolated Windows account or practice profile for each participant, disable syncing and recording, and keep the computer under supervision. Never open a personal profile. Close the practice launcher between participants and remove the dedicated practice profile according to the approved retention plan. DPAPI does not protect against other programs running as that Windows user. Do not describe file deletion as forensic erasure. Store any approved anonymous observations separately from application data. Report adverse events and unexpected disclosures to the approved contact.
